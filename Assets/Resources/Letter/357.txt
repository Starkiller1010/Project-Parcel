Hey, this is a letter from Blackmailer to Wife.

Would you like to know what your husband
has been up to? Would you like to let in
on the secrets that he carries behind
your back? Ask him or any one that knows
him what he truly is and watch the world
you know unravel.

If you want to protect him still, you only
have to let us in and show you the powers
of our leader and the righteous of the sacred.
